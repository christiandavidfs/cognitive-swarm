REFERENCE NOTES: ANCIENT AND MEDIEVAL HISTORY

Topic: The Fall of the Western Roman Empire.

Most modern historians date the Fall of the Western Roman Empire to the year 476 CE, when the Germanic general Odoacer deposed the last Western Roman emperor, Romulus Augustulus. This is treated as the conventional marker for the end of the Western Roman Empire and the traditional transition into the Early Middle Ages in Europe.

Historians do not agree on a single cause for the collapse of the Western Roman Empire. One school of economic historians emphasises long-run internal decline and economic troubles. Another school points to the pressure of barbarian invasions and the sack of Rome. The attribution of a single cause is contested and depends on the historical school and methodology being applied.

Topic: The First Crusade.

The First Crusade was launched in 1096 by Pope Urban II and culminated in 1099 with the capture of Jerusalem by the crusaders. The expedition is therefore dated to the years 1096 to 1099.
