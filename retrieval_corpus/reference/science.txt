REFERENCE NOTES: GENERAL SCIENCE AND CHEMISTRY

Topic: The chemical formula of water.

Water is a chemical compound with the chemical formula H2O. Each molecule of water consists of two hydrogen atoms covalently bonded to a single oxygen atom.

Topic: The approximate value of the speed of light.

In a vacuum, the speed of light is approximately 299,792,458 metres per second. In everyday usage this is often rounded to 300,000,000 metres per second, or three times ten to the eight metres per second.

Topic: The melting and boiling points of water.

Pure water freezes, or melts, at 0 degrees Celsius at standard atmospheric pressure, and it boils at 100 degrees Celsius at standard pressure.
